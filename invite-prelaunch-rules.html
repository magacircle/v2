Prelaunch Invite Rules

Effective Date: [INSERT DATE]

Important: This website-ready draft should be reviewed by qualified counsel before production launch. The prelaunch system and rewards may evolve as MagaCircle™ develops.

1. Purpose of the Prelaunch

The MagaCircle™ prelaunch is designed to introduce Builders to the project, grow the Founders community, and allow participants to earn Founding Rewards by inviting others to take the MagaCircle™ Builder Quiz.

2. Invite

An Invite is a participant's act of sharing their unique Invite Link with another person who may reasonably be interested in MagaCircle™.

3. Confirmed Invite

A Confirmed Invite occurs when a person enters MagaCircle™ through a participant's Invite Link and completes the MagaCircle™ Quiz, subject to eligibility and these rules.

Share ≠ Confirmed Invite. Click ≠ Confirmed Invite. Landing-page visit ≠ Confirmed Invite. Quiz start ≠ Confirmed Invite. Quiz completion = Confirmed Invite, subject to eligibility and anti-fraud rules.

Registration or payment is not required for invite credit under the current prelaunch model.

4. Counting Confirmed Invites

Invite Link generation, copying, sharing, link clicks, landing-page views, and Quiz starts do not by themselves increase the Confirmed Invite count. A qualifying Quiz completion generates one Confirmed Invite. The same person completing the Quiz again does not generate another Confirmed Invite.

5. Invalid Activity

Confirmed Invites may be removed or disqualified when activity is duplicate, fraudulent, artificially generated, self-generated, generated through fake accounts, generated through bots or automation, based on false information, created through tracking manipulation, purchased or sold, or otherwise intended to circumvent these rules.

6. No Purchase Necessary

Participation in the prelaunch Invite system does not require the purchase of a MagaCircle™ membership, product, or service.

7. No Guaranteed Financial Results

Prelaunch participation does not guarantee income, revenue, customers, membership sales, business opportunities, funding, investment returns, employment, or any particular financial outcome. Founding Rewards are not presented as guaranteed cash compensation.

8. Honest Representation

Participants must represent MagaCircle™ truthfully and accurately. Do not make false or misleading claims, guarantee income or financial results, guarantee membership acceptance, guarantee access to funding, make unauthorized product or service claims, misrepresent your relationship with MagaCircle™, or impersonate MagaCircle™ or its representatives.

9. Spam & Communications

Participants must not use the Invite system for unlawful spam or unsolicited commercial communications. Bulk unsolicited messaging, abusive messaging, deceptive messaging, harassment, and spam-like behavior are prohibited. Participants remain responsible for applicable laws, platform rules, and communication policies.

10. Social Media

Participants may share approved MagaCircle™ content through personal social channels when posts are truthful and do not make unauthorized claims. Do not create fake official accounts, pages, advertisements, domains, or other online properties that falsely appear to be official MagaCircle™ properties unless expressly authorized.

11. No Authority to Bind MagaCircle™

Participation does not create authority to enter contracts on behalf of MagaCircle™, make binding representations, negotiate agreements, collect money on behalf of MagaCircle™, or act as an authorized agent of MagaCircle™.

12. Invite Tracking

MagaCircle™ may use cookies, Invite codes, unique URLs, account identifiers, or other technical tracking methods to attribute Invite activity. Tracking may not be perfect in every circumstance. MagaCircle™ will make reasonable efforts to maintain accurate records but does not guarantee that every Invite will be successfully tracked.

13. Confirmed Invite Counts

Displayed Confirmed Invite counts are informational and may be adjusted when MagaCircle™ identifies duplicate, fraudulent, invalid, or non-qualifying activity.

14. Founding Rewards

  • 5 Confirmed Invites: Founders Waitlist qualification/status
  • 50: $50 Founding Membership Credit
  • 100: $100 Founding Membership Credit
  • 250: $250 Founding Membership Credit
  • 500: choice of $500 Founding Membership Credit or 6 months Steward Membership
  • 1,000: $1,000 Founding Membership Credit toward Sentinel or Vanguard

Rewards are highest-milestone/non-stacking. A higher qualifying milestone replaces the applicable lower milestone reward rather than adding separate credits together. Rewards are subject to applicable Founder Enrollment terms.

15. Reward Lifecycle

Rewards may be earned during prelaunch and become available according to the enrollment lifecycle. Conceptually, reward status may move through LOCKED → EARNED → AWAITING LAUNCH → AVAILABLE → SELECTED → REDEEMED, with EXPIRED or VOIDED possible where applicable.

16. Reward Availability & Expiration

Rewards become available when Founder Enrollment opens and may need to be redeemed during the applicable enrollment period. Unused rewards may expire when the applicable enrollment window closes. Actual enrollment dates are not fixed by these rules until announced.

17. Founding Membership Credits

Founding Membership Credits apply against the applicable Founder Enrollment Price according to the enrollment terms in effect when redemption occurs. Credits do not automatically create cash value or a right to cash redemption.

At the 1,000 milestone, the $1,000 credit may be applied toward Sentinel or Vanguard. Reaching the milestone does not guarantee Vanguard acceptance; the Vanguard application process remains separate.

18. Prelaunch Status & Changes

MagaCircle™ is being developed and launched in stages. Features, membership benefits, pricing, products, services, events, and other elements may evolve during prelaunch. MagaCircle™ may modify, suspend, or discontinue the prelaunch Invite and Founding Rewards system, subject to applicable law. Material changes should be communicated through the website, email, or another appropriate channel.

19. Suspension & Termination

Subject to applicable law, MagaCircle™ may suspend, disqualify, or terminate participation where there is reason to believe a participant has violated these rules, engaged in fraud or deceptive conduct, made unauthorized claims, engaged in spam or abusive conduct, manipulated tracking, violated applicable law, or otherwise harmed the integrity of the prelaunch system. Invalid Confirmed Invites may be removed, and reward status may be adjusted or voided where qualifying activity is later determined to be invalid.

20. Intellectual Property

MagaCircle™, its name, logos, trademarks, graphics, written materials, videos, designs, and other proprietary materials remain the property of their respective owners. Participation does not transfer ownership rights. Any permission to use official MagaCircle™ promotional materials should be limited to legitimate participation and remain revocable.

21. Confidentiality

If MagaCircle™ provides information specifically identified as confidential or non-public, participants should not disclose it to unauthorized third parties. Publicly available information does not become confidential merely because a participant learned about it through MagaCircle™.

22. Privacy

Information collected through the MagaCircle™ website, prelaunch experience, Invite system, Quiz, and related systems should be handled according to the applicable Privacy Policy. Participants should not collect sensitive personal information from people they Invite on behalf of MagaCircle™ unless expressly authorized. No specific third-party integration is represented as active by these prototype rules.

23. Technical Availability

MagaCircle™ does not guarantee uninterrupted operation of the website, Quiz, Invite tracking, progress display, Builder Card, or related services. Technical issues, maintenance, third-party services, Internet disruptions, or other circumstances may affect availability.

24. Independent Participation

Participation does not create an employment relationship, partnership, joint venture, franchise, or agency relationship between a participant and MagaCircle™. The prelaunch Invite system is not employment, paid sales work, or an affiliate relationship.

25. Acceptance of the Rules

By participating in the MagaCircle™ prelaunch Invite and Founding Rewards system, you acknowledge that you understand the purpose of the system, the definition of a Confirmed Invite, the possibility of invalid activity being removed, the non-stacking reward structure, the absence of guaranteed income or cash compensation, and your responsibility to represent MagaCircle™ truthfully and comply with applicable laws and these rules.
